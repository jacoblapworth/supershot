### Description

Keep the score. Control the clock. Stay with the game.

Supershot is a fast, focused netball scorer built for umpires and everyone trusted with match-day timing. Set up the teams, choose the first centre pass and run all four quarters from one clear courtside view.

Scoring stays simple when the pressure is on. Add a goal with one tap, undo a mistake instantly and always know which team has the next centre pass. Supershot keeps the quarter timer, breaks and scoreboard together, so your attention stays where it belongs: on court.

BUILT FOR MATCH DAY

• Score goals with a single tap  
• Undo the latest goal instantly  
• Track the next centre pass  
• Confirm whether the final centre pass was taken at quarter time  
• Start, pause and resume the match timer  
• Move smoothly through quarters and breaks

YOUR GAME, YOUR TIMING

Set the length of each quarter, then choose one break duration or customise every interval—including half time. Add your teams, choose their colours and match the scoreboard to the bibs on court.

A RECORD WORTH KEEPING

Every completed match becomes part of your history. Revisit the final result, scores by quarter and a chronological goal timeline. Team pages keep past games together, ready whenever you want to look back.

GO FURTHER WITH SUPERSHOT PRO

Supershot Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks:

• Live Activities for the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island  
• Timer controls from the Live Activity  
• Quarter-end alerts, even when Supershot is not on screen  
• Goal-progression charts  
• Centre-pass conversion insights  
• Inferred turnovers  
• Average time-to-goal statistics

Core scoring, timing, teams and match history remain free.

No advertising. No marketing tracking. Purchase processing may involve Apple and our purchase provider as described in the privacy policy.

From first centre pass to final whistle, Supershot keeps every quarter on track.
